ABOUT

I build local-first software for the Solana Mobile ecosystem (Solana Seeker): apps that store and process data on-device, sign through Seed Vault and Mobile Wallet Adapter (MWA), and keep your private keys in hardware.

I care about what the industry often ignores — real usage telemetry, battery accuracy, and privacy by default. Every project ships without cloud dependencies or analytics. Wallet features go through Seed Vault on demand — read-only balances where possible, transaction signing only with your explicit approval, and support for .skr domains.
