PRIVACY POLICY

LAST UPDATED: SEPTEMBER 2026 · APPLIES TO ALL SHEVCHUKIHOR APPLICATIONS

1. Core Principle

ShevchukIhor applications are local-first: your data lives on your device. No analytics, no crash reporting, no telemetry, no accounts.

2. Data Stored On-Device

Apps may store, entirely locally, data such as: app usage statistics (foreground time, launch counts), battery samples, and wallet connection details (your public address, optional label). Exactly what each App stores is detailed in that App's own Privacy Policy.

3. Wallet & Blockchain

When you connect a wallet, the App performs read-only queries to public Solana RPC endpoints using your public address. Private keys and seed phrases never leave the Seed Vault hardware enclave. Transactions require your explicit approval.

4. Data Sharing

Your data is never sold, rented, or shared with third parties. The only external communication is read-only blockchain/API queries using your public address.

5. Your Control

Because data stays on your device, you fully control it: view it in the App, clear it, or uninstall the App to erase everything.

6. Changes

This policy may be updated. Changes are reflected on this page.
